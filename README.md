# CertIQ — AI
**Turning product performance signals into actionable decisions.**

CertIQ is an AI-powered product intelligence portal designed to help product teams identify performance risks, investigate underlying causes, discover opportunities, and take data-driven action.

Rather than simply displaying performance metrics, CertIQ connects analysis to execution and measures the impact of implemented actions over time.

> **Demo notice:** This portfolio demonstrates the product experience using sample data. No confidential company or customer information is included.

## Product Overview

Product teams often have access to dashboards and performance reports, but identifying why performance is declining, deciding what to do next, and measuring whether an intervention worked can require significant manual effort.

CertIQ aims to connect these activities in one continuous workflow.

## How It Works

1. **Detect performance risks** — AI continuously analyses product performance and identifies signs of declining performance or potential risk.
2. **Investigate root causes** — AI agents analyse relevant data and signals to identify underlying reasons and contributing factors.
3. **Recommend actions** — Agents turn their findings into actionable improvement recommendations.
4. **Manage execution** — Users add recommendations to their personal task list, set deadlines, and manage implementation.
5. **Measure impact** — After an action is completed, AI agents monitor relevant business KPIs, compare performance before and after implementation, and assess its impact.

## Key Capabilities

- Product performance monitoring
- Automated risk detection and alerts
- AI-agent-led investigation
- Data-driven recommendations
- Personal action and task management
- Post-implementation KPI monitoring
- Before-and-after performance assessment

## Product Thinking

CertIQ is designed around a simple principle: product intelligence should not stop at reporting what happened. It should help teams understand why it happened, decide what to do, and evaluate the outcome.

The product connects insight, decision-making, execution, and measurement into one workflow.

## Product Design Principles

CertIQ was built around four core principles:

**1. Move from monitoring to intelligence**

Go beyond displaying performance metrics. Identify emerging risks, declining trends, and opportunities that require attention.

**2. Move from insights to investigation**

Use AI agents to investigate signals across relevant data sources, uncover contributing factors, and provide context behind performance changes.

**3. Connect recommendations to execution**

Make insights actionable by allowing users to add recommended improvements to their task lists and manage their implementation.

**4. Close the loop with impact measurement**

Continue monitoring product performance after implementation, compare KPIs before and after an action, and assess whether the intervention made a difference.

The objective is to create a continuous cycle of detection, investigation, decision-making, execution, and measurement.
